Who I Am

I'm a data analyst with 15 years of experience in mortgage finance and consumer products. Today I'm the Operations / Data & Compliance Manager at First Rate Financial, where I build the forecasting models, dashboards and reporting tools that sales and executive leadership rely on every day.

What drives me is the practical side of data. Not data for its own sake, but data that answers a real business question: how much demand a market supports, what a rate change does to volume, or where next quarter is headed. Most of my work starts with collecting and cleaning raw data and ends in something a business user opens.

My Background

My career started at Blue Bell Creameries, where I managed a team of ten and a nine-route distribution network across three states. I didn't study that network from a desk. I rode the routes, learned the real constraints firsthand, and built the business case for three new routes. It was there that I first saw how powerfully data could shape operational decisions, and I've been chasing that ever since.

From there I moved into mortgage finance. At Citizens Mortgage I was promoted from underwriting to Inside Sales Analyst, reporting to C-suite executives on loan volume, market trends and competitors, and contributing to $540 million in annual volume growth. At First Rate Financial I've spent the years since building the reporting and forecasting behind about $200 million in funded loans a year, more than $1 billion in all.

Where I'm Headed

I went back to school for a Master of Science in Applied Data Science at Syracuse University, where I spent most of my time on machine learning: building neural networks, tuning predictive models and validating them against real data. Now I want to put those skills to work on forecasting, analytics and revenue strategy at a larger scale.

Model validation, back-testing and explaining results in plain business language are things I've been doing for years. I'm looking for the team where they matter most.
