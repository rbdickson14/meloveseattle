Background

Where Strategy Meets Data

My career path has not been a traditional one: five years running a sales territory for Blue Bell Creameries, then a decade in mortgage finance with Citizens and First Rate Financial. The common thread is the questions I get to answer with data. How much demand can a market support? How will a rate change move loan volume? Where is next quarter's production headed?

Today I am the Operations / Data & Compliance Manager at First Rate Financial in Dallas. I build the forecasting models and dashboards that serve as the single source of reporting for sales and executive leadership, I automated our state and federal regulatory reporting, and we have kept perfect scores on every state and federal exam.
